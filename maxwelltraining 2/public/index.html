Questions.

How is this different from programs I find online?

Programs online are built for everyone, which means they're built for no one. This program is written around your specific answers — your sport, equipment, schedule, experience level, injuries, and goals. Two athletes with different profiles receive completely different programs.

How long does it take to get my program?

Your program is ready in about 60 seconds. You can preview the full thing before paying, then download the PDF instantly after checkout.

Do I need a gym?

No. The quiz asks about your equipment. No equipment = 100% bodyweight program. Home gym = dumbbells and pull-up bar only. Full gym = everything. The program adapts completely.

I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?

Yes. Beginners get completely different exercises. Goblet squats instead of barbell squats. Lat pulldowns instead of weighted pull-ups. DB deadlifts instead of heavy conventional pulls. Nothing complex until you're ready for it.

What's actually in the PDF?

Cover page with your starting weights and weekly schedule. One page per training day with every exercise, exact weight, sets, reps, rest time, and coaching cue. Nutrition targets. Full periodized progression. Coaching notes written for you. For 12-week programs that's typically 40-60 pages.

I play a sport. Will it account for that?

Yes. The quiz asks your sport, position, and season. Soccer players get Nordic curls, Copenhagen planks, hip thrusts, and conditioning. In-season programs reduce volume so you're not dead before game day.

Can I do calisthenics only?

Yes. Select Calisthenics as your training style and your program is entirely bodyweight — push-up progressions, pull-up progressions, pistol squat work, L-sits, dips. No weights, even with a full gym.

Can I edit my program after I get it?

Yes. After your program is generated, there's an adjust panel where you can change workout length, equipment, exercises, volume, or any other preference before you pay. The program regenerates instantly.

What if I'm not happy with my program?

Message me within 7 days. If the program wasn't personalized to your answers or you're unhappy for any reason, I'll make it right. Maxwell.ionita@gmail.com

Is nutrition included?

Yes. Every program includes daily protein, calorie, carb, and fat targets calculated from your bodyweight and goals. Meal timing guidance is included in every program.
